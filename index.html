    How it works

    It knows the price, the deadlines and when to hand off.

    The agent works from the inquiry record and Albion's published facts. It never invents an aid figure or a deadline, and anything personal, from a family's finances to a recruit's eligibility, goes to a counselor or coach with the conversation attached.

    Admission facts

    The new price, explained one to one

    $38,000 tuition from fall 2027, the roughly $19,000 average net cost, merit scholarship tiers, the Visit Award and the applied-learning guarantee.

    Every channel

    Text, RCS, web chat, email and calls

    One conversation per family across all of them, from a verified Albion College sender, with a human-sounding call when a parent asks for one.

    Books the moments that matter

    Visits, FAFSA, deposits

    Department Days, Saturday visits, Fall Preview Day and coach visits booked in the thread, then reminders and summer-checklist nudges.

    Works for counselors and coaches

    Handoffs with context

    Aid appeals, eligibility questions and anything emotional go to a person. Counselors see the transcript and the next step.

    Why follow-up is the whole game

    One enrolled student is worth about $75,000.

    Albion says the average family pays roughly $19,000 a year after aid. Over four years that is about $75,000 of net revenue per student. "Flat is the new growth," and every inquiry that goes quiet is a seat that has to be refilled.

    $38,000published tuition starting fall 2027, down from the current sticker
    $2,000 / yrVisit Award for admitted students who visit before April 30 of senior year
    24 teamsNCAA Division III programs, each recruiting large rosters by personal follow-up

    Figures as published on albion.edu in October 2026. The per-student value is 21X's estimate from Albion's published average net cost, not an Albion figure.
